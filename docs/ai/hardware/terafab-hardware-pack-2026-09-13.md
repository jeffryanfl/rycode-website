# Terafab — Hardware learning pack (for Architect /ai)
**Date:** 13 Sep 2026  
**Lane:** `/ai` Hardware learning hub (facts), not a long essay  
**Spelling:** **Terafab** (one word). Confirmed on company site https://terafab.ai/ and SpaceX Updates https://www.spacex.com/updates/terafab  
**Hard distinction:** Terafab is a **planned semiconductor fabrication plant (chip fab)**. It is **not** an AI training data center. The Musk-orbit training campus people often confuse with it is **Colossus** (Memphis / Southaven) under SpaceXAI / former xAI.

## 2) Public facts vs rumor / unstable numbers

### Treat as **public / company-stated** (cite company or filing-backed press)
- Official name **Terafab**; marketing site live at https://terafab.ai/
- Grimes County, TX selected for scale fab (Aug 2026 SpaceX announcement)
- Phase 1 ~$16.8B; ≥3,000 jobs; >100M sq ft planned (SpaceX Updates)
- Research fab precursor at Giga Texas North Campus (SpaceX Updates)
- Purpose includes edge/inference silicon for Tesla robots/vehicles **and** chips for space-based data centers (SpaceX Updates)
- Texas Enterprise Fund / JETI mentions appear in state/development press quoting Abbott — treat as **reported incentive package**, verify against Texas primary before site copy if used

### Treat as **filing / earlier plan — supersession risk**
- May 2026 Grimes County public-hearing / tax-abatement reporting: phase ~**$55B**, full buildout up to ~**$119B** (CNBC, The Register summarizing county notice). **Do not mix with $16.8B** without saying “earlier filing vs later company announcement.”
- Intel as co-developer / 14A node: appears in secondary press and Wikipedia; **not** in Aug 2026 SpaceX Terafab announcement text. Label **unconfirmed / dropped from later company text** until Intel or SpaceX primary confirms.
- Process node claims (e.g. “2nm”) in secondary outlets: **hold** without foundry/company primary.

### Treat as **ambition / not operational fact**
- “Largest chip manufacturing facility on the planet” / 50× global production / galactic Type I–III framing on terafab.ai
- Orbital data centers as economic premise for fab demand — **roadmap claim**, not a present Terafab operating status
- Wafer starts/month, yield, process node, first silicon date for Grimes: **not on SpaceX Terafab page** as of this pack → **open**

### Related but **different project**: Colossus (training campus)
- Operational AI training / inference campus in Memphis TN / Southaven MS area; GPU clusters, onsite/adjacent generation, cooling — **data center**, not fab
- Epoch AI estimates (methodology-based, not company S-1): Colossus 1 ~**340 MW IT**, ~**230k GPUs / 276k H100-eq**, ~$12.9B; Colossus 2 ~**946 MW IT** (Epoch directory). Anthropic May 2026: >300 MW / >220k GPUs on Colossus 1 (company announcement via Epoch citation)
- Scientific American / local reporting: large portable gas-turbine fleet ~**1,775 MW** capacity by Jul 2026 for power side — **power plant / generation story**, separate from Terafab

## 4) Five–eight bullets Architect can turn into Hardware learning pages

1. **Fab ≠ campus** — Terafab makes chips; Colossus (and Stargate-class sites) *consume* chips and power. Separate learning pages or a clear hub diagram.
2. **Official spelling & owners** — Terafab; SpaceX + Tesla on the Aug 2026 announcement; marketing site terafab.ai.
3. **Location stack** — Research fab at Giga Texas North Campus (started Apr 2026 per SpaceX) → scale fab Grimes County, TX.
4. **Vertical integration claim** — Logic + memory + advanced packaging (+ test) in one complex vs TSMC’s foundry model (logic fabs + separate packaging projects in Arizona).
5. **Capex honesty page** — Teach readers to separate **$16.8B phase-1 company figure** from **$55B / $119B earlier filing figures**; both can be true as phase vs full-build scenarios, but must be labeled.
6. **Demand drivers** — Edge silicon (Optimus / vehicles) vs high-power silicon for **space-based data centers** — two product classes on one fab roadmap (company claim).
7. **Peer scale (fab)** — TSMC Arizona: $265B planned, 10 fabs, >1,100 acres, Fab1 in HVM — shows what a *proven* US leading-edge buildout looks like vs Terafab’s announced footprint.
8. **Peer scale (campus)** — Colossus 1 ~340 MW IT / ~230k GPUs (Epoch); Abilene Stargate-class ~1.2 GW OpenAI/Oracle path (press) — context for *why* captive silicon ambitions exist, without calling Terafab a data center.

## 5) Open questions / what not to claim

**Do not claim:**
- Terafab is operational or already producing leading-edge wafers at Grimes
- Terafab *is* the Memphis data center / Colossus
- Exact process node, wafer starts, yields, or first-production date (unless new primary appears)
- That Intel is a confirmed joint owner (absent from later SpaceX Terafab announcement)
- That $16.8B and $119B are the same “cost of Terafab” without phase language
- That “1 TW of compute per year” equals 1 TW of grid power at one site
- Any GRC / permitting compliance verdict (out of scope)

- How much of Grimes output is reserved for orbital vs terrestrial
- Water, power, and environmental permitting status for Grimes (local primary)
- Whether research fab at Giga Texas has published wafer capacity (~“few thousand wafers/month” appeared in earnings-call secondary — verify transcript before use)
